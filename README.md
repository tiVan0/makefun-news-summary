A narrative summary of the world's news of Wednesday 11 November 2026 — the day's most consequential stories, told as a story rather than a wire list: the Abu Dhabi peace talks ending without a deal, the COP31 leaders' summit opening in Antalya, a day of espionage and terror charges, Nvidia's boardroom crisis, Spain's first general strike in 14 years, and a building collapse in Brooklyn. Every fact is sourced (Reuters, AFP, AP, the New York Times, the Wall Street Journal, the Justice Department) and verified against reporting from 11 November itself.

## Run it

```sh
mkdir -p out && MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR=out python3 main.py
```

The program copies `summary.txt` to `$MAKEFUN_OUTPUT_DIR/entry.txt` (or `/output/entry.txt`), byte for byte, UTF-8, 1,967 characters — inside the 1–2,000 gate. Two runs write identical bytes.
