Wednesday 11 November 2026 brought no breakthrough in Ukraine, a stark warning at the climate summit in Turkey, and a day of arrests, strikes and a boardroom crisis.

In Abu Dhabi, the second day of US-mediated peace talks between Ukraine and Russia ended after about two hours with no ceasefire or deal, though both sides agreed to a large exchange of prisoners of war. American envoys Steve Witkoff and Jared Kushner were due in Moscow on Thursday to meet Vladimir Putin, Reuters reported.

In Antalya, world leaders opened the COP31 climate summit, where UN Secretary-General António Guterres warned that the present course amounted to 'mutually assured destruction', with 2025 emissions at a record and the past four years the hottest on record, according to AFP. The United States was absent for a second year.

The US Justice Department charged three men it called Iranian operatives over nearly 20 alleged plots in America and Europe, including one at RAF Fairford in England. Treasury Secretary Scott Bessent was also moved to a military base after threats on his life, the New York Times reported. And British police arrested a 41-year-old US Army officer serving with NATO's Allied Rapid Reaction Corps near Gloucester on suspicion of spying for Russia; he has not been charged or named, the Associated Press said.

In business, Nvidia's board was preparing to remove chief executive Jensen Huang, the Wall Street Journal reported, after reports of a second affair, this time with a company executive; shares fell about 8% after hours. In Spain, millions of workers staged the country's first general strike in 14 years over housing, where the average rent of €1,176 now takes 98.7% of a young worker's monthly pay, Reuters reported.

In Brooklyn, a three-storey apartment building collapsed into a construction site, killing at least two people and injuring 17, among them seven children; rescuers were still searching the rubble, the fire department said.
